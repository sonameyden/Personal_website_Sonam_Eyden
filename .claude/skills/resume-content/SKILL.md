# Writing resume content

The owner's real experience is the only source. Make it clear and specific;
never make it bigger than it is.

## Sources and truth

- Start from `resume-source/MASTER_FACTS.md`: every CV merged, with each
  detail's source noted. The original CVs are in `resume-source/cvs/`. Its
  "Decisions" section records the owner's rulings where CVs disagree; they
  override every CV. Never publish the phone number.
- Take facts from what the owner gives you: their CV, LinkedIn text, project
  repositories and READMEs, or their answers. Note where each fact came from
  when it is not obvious.
- Do not invent or round up: no made-up metrics ("improved performance by
  40%"), team sizes, users, awards, employers, dates or technologies. If a
  bullet would be stronger with a number, ask the owner for the real one.
- A project that is a prototype, a proposal, coursework or a demo is
  described as that. Say what was built and what is planned, separately.
- Keep private things private: no home address, ID numbers, client names or
  confidential project details unless the owner says they may be public.

## Writing

- Headline: name, role or the role being sought, and one line on what the
  owner does well. Plain words, no buzzword stacks.
- Experience and project bullets: action verb, what was done, with what, and
  the result or scope when it is real. One line each where possible; three to
  five bullets per role.
- Projects: one sentence on the problem, one on what the owner built and
  their part in it, the stack, and links (live demo, repository) that work.
- Skills: group them (languages, frameworks, tools, domains) and list only
  what the owner can talk about in an interview. No percentage bars or
  star ratings.
- Same tense, date format and spelling style throughout. Check spelling.
- Write for a reader skimming in under a minute: the most relevant items
  first, short paragraphs, no filler.

## Before you say it is done

1. Every claim traces back to something the owner provided or confirmed.
2. List the questions you still have for the owner (missing dates, real
   numbers, links) instead of filling the gaps yourself.
